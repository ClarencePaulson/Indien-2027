# The Taj Mahal Palace, Mumbai -- Club-benefits

Gäller Taj Club Rooms och Executive Suites (Palace Wing). Sparat 2026-09-30.

## Originaltext

Taj Club Rooms and Suites:
- One-way transfer from the airport to the hotel for all reservations booked on Taj Club Rooms & Executive Suite
- Access to buffet breakfast at the Sea Lounge or Palace Lounge (between 0730 hrs to 1030hrs)
- Service of hot and soft beverages at the Palace Lounge from 0700 hrs to 2300 hrs and 24-hour personalized floor butler service.
- Hi-tea at the Palace Lounge from 1500 hrs to 1700 hrs
- Daily evening cocktail hours at the Palace Lounge from 1800 hrs to 2000 hrs
- 2 pieces of garments for ironing per room, per night convenience of early check-in (post 0900 hrs) and late check-out (until 1800 hrs), subject to availability
- Access to a fully equipped fitness centre and steam room
- Standard Wi-Fi for up to 4 devices
